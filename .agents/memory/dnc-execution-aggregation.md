---
name: DNC execution aggregation
description: How multi-participant field and classroom executions relate to individual DNC annual programs.
---

Record a field, classroom, or workshop execution once at the DNC request level. Add its duration once to the linked annual program; keep the participant count as separate attendance context and keep simulator sessions in their own records.

**Why:** A DNC request addresses one operator's gap but its delivery can include a group. Multiplying duration by attendance would inflate that operator's yearly training hours, while reusing simulator records would make it difficult to distinguish delivery methods.

**How to apply:** Use request-level execution records for group delivery tied to DNC annual programs. Use individual training activities for separately assigned per-operator learning, and do not count the same execution in both sources.
